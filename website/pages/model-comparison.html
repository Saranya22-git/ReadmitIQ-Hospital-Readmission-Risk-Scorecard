Why XGBoost Was Selected

Model Selection Rationale
⚡
Gradient Boosting
XGBoost builds trees sequentially, each correcting errors of the last. This reduces both bias and variance better than a single tree or bagging approach.
🏥
Handles Mixed Data
Medical datasets mix binary flags (CHF, COPD), continuous values (age, LOS), and categorical features — XGBoost handles all natively without scaling.
🔍
SHAP Compatible
Tree-based SHAP runs in exact O(TLD) time on XGBoost models — giving per-patient explainability that clinicians and regulators require.
