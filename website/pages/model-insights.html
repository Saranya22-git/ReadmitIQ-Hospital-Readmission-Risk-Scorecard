About SHAP Explainability

Model Transparency
What is SHAP?
SHapley Additive exPlanations assigns each feature a contribution score for every prediction, grounded in cooperative game theory.
Why it Matters
Clinicians need to trust predictions. SHAP shows exactly which patient factors drove the risk score — making the model auditable.
Reading the Chart
Higher SHAP value = stronger average impact on predictions. Age, medication count, and prior admissions dominate readmission risk.
